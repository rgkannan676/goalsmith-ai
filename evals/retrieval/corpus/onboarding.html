Onboarding Guide for New Joiners

Welcome to Lumen Labs. This guide walks you through your first weeks.

Before your first day

You will receive an email with your username and a link to set up your password and security key. Your laptop is shipped to your home address five working days before you start.

Your first week

Day one starts with a welcome session at 09:30 run by People Operations. During the week you meet your manager for a goals conversation, complete the mandatory security and privacy training, and get access to the tools your team uses.

Buddy programme

Every new joiner is paired with a buddy from another team. Your buddy is your first contact for informal questions, such as where to find documents or how meetings work, and meets you weekly during your first two months.

Probation period

The probation period is six months. Your manager holds check-ins at the end of month one, month three and month five. Either side can end the contract with two weeks' notice during probation.
