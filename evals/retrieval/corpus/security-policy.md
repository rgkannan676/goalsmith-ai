# Information Security Policy

Owner: Security team. Applies to employees, contractors and interns.

## Accounts and passwords

Passwords must be at least 14 characters and are stored only in the company password manager. Never reuse a work password on another site. Multi-factor authentication is required on every company account; hardware security keys are issued to engineers and finance staff.

## Lost or stolen devices

If a laptop or phone with company data is lost or stolen, report it to the security desk within one hour by calling extension 4400 or posting in #sec-urgent. The device will be wiped remotely. A police report is needed for insurance if the device was stolen.

## Data classification

We use four levels. Public data may be shared freely. Internal data stays inside the company. Confidential data, such as customer contracts, is limited to the teams that need it. Restricted data, such as payment card numbers and health records, may only be stored in systems approved by the Security team and must be encrypted at rest.

## Access reviews

Managers review their team's access to production systems every quarter. Access that is not confirmed within 10 working days is removed automatically. Leavers lose all access on their last working day.

## Reporting a security incident

Report suspected phishing, malware or a data leak right away through the "Report incident" button in the security portal. Do not try to investigate on your own and do not delete the suspicious email. The security on-call engineer responds within 30 minutes during working hours.

## Software installation

Only install software from the company app catalogue. Requests for other tools go through a ticket in the IT service desk; the Security team reviews them within five working days. Browser extensions that can read all websites are not allowed.
