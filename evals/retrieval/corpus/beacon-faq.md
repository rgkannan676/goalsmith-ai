# Beacon FAQ

Beacon is Lumen Labs' indoor air-quality sensor and monitoring app.

## Installation

Mount the Beacon sensor at head height, away from windows and heaters. Install the Beacon app, hold the button on the sensor for five seconds until the light blinks blue, and follow the pairing steps. One account can manage up to 50 sensors.

## Supported platforms

The Beacon app runs on iOS 17 or later and Android 13 or later. The web dashboard supports the latest two versions of Chrome, Firefox, Safari and Edge. There is no desktop app.

## Error codes

### E-1042

E-1042 means the sensor cannot reach the Wi-Fi network. Beacon only supports 2.4 GHz networks; check that the network is not 5 GHz only, then restart the sensor.

### E-2077

E-2077 means the CO2 sensor needs calibration. Place the sensor outdoors or by an open window for 20 minutes and press "Calibrate" in the app.

## Battery

The built-in battery lasts about 18 months with default settings. Measuring every minute instead of every five minutes cuts battery life to roughly 6 months. The app warns you when 10 percent is left.

## Pricing

Beacon Home costs 129 euros per sensor with no subscription. Beacon Business costs 99 euros per sensor plus 4 euros per sensor per month, which adds the multi-site dashboard, alerts by SMS and API access.

## Data retention

Measurements are kept for 24 months on Beacon Home and for 7 years on Beacon Business. You can export all your data as CSV at any time from the dashboard settings, and deleting your account erases all measurements within 30 days.
