# Lumen Labs Employee Handbook

This handbook explains how we work at Lumen Labs. It applies to all full-time employees.

## Working hours

### Core hours

Our core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in your local time zone. Outside core hours you may arrange your day as you like, as long as you work 40 hours a week on average.

### Remote work

Every employee may work remotely up to three days a week. Fully remote arrangements need written approval from your director and are reviewed each year. You must work from a country where Lumen Labs has a registered entity; working from other countries for more than 14 days needs approval from People Operations.

## Leave

### Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, plus public holidays. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year and must be used by 31 March.

### Parental leave

Employees who become parents through birth, adoption or surrogacy receive 18 weeks of fully paid parental leave. It can be taken in up to three blocks within the first 12 months. Tell your manager at least 8 weeks before the leave starts.

### Sick leave

You can take up to 10 days of paid sick leave per year without a doctor's note. From the fourth consecutive sick day you need a medical certificate, uploaded to the HR portal.

## Equipment

New employees choose a 14-inch or 16-inch laptop on their first day. Laptops are replaced every three years. You can also claim a one-time home office allowance of 600 euros for a desk, chair or monitor.

## Expenses

Submit expense claims within 30 days of the purchase using form EX-12 in the finance portal. Attach an itemised receipt for anything over 25 euros. Claims are reimbursed with the next monthly salary.

## Learning budget

Each employee has a yearly learning budget of 1,500 euros for courses, books and conferences. Unused budget does not carry over. Conference trips also need travel approval.
