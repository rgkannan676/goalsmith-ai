Business Travel Policy

All business travel must be approved by your manager before you book. Book through the TravelDesk portal so that trips are covered by our insurance.

Flights

Book economy class for flights shorter than six hours. Premium economy is allowed for longer flights. Business class needs approval from a vice president and is only for flights longer than ten hours.

Book at least 14 days before departure where possible; last-minute bookings need a short justification.

Hotels

Hotel rates are capped per night: 160 euros in most European cities, 220 euros in London, Paris and Zurich, and 280 dollars in New York and San Francisco. Stays above the cap need approval from Finance.

Per diem and meals

When travelling you receive a daily allowance of 45 euros for meals, or 25 euros when breakfast is included in the hotel rate. Alcohol is never reimbursed.

Ground transport

  • Use public transport or the airport train when practical.

  • Taxis and ride-hailing are fine late at night or when carrying equipment.
  • Rental cars need manager approval; book the smallest suitable category.

International travel

For trips outside the European Union, check visa requirements at least four weeks ahead and register the trip in the TravelDesk safety tracker. Laptops taken to high-risk countries must be loaner devices from IT.
